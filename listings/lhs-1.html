Community, Ownership & Options

Las Huertas combines clean-title land with an HOA and controlled development plan. Buyers can choose bare land, multi-unit lots, or turnkey and pre-sale homes, with developer financing advertised for up to 24 months. The community is approximately a 5–7 minute walk from the Playa Saladita waves.

Planned and operating amenities include Hacienda Plaza—with a gym, market, surf shop, healthy store, office and jiu-jitsu studio—plus daycare, Casa Mango, pickleball, a clubhouse, community farms, green areas and a skate miniramp.

Community standards include underground utilities, limits on building height and footprint, required green space, native and fruit-tree planting, and responsible waste and recycling practices. Mixed-use and lower-density residential lot options are available; current measurements, construction allowances, HOA fees and inventory should be confirmed directly.
